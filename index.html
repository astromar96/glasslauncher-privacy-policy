Glass Launcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Glass Launcher is a home screen replacement for Android, published by Pixelz Apps. This policy explains exactly what the app does with data on your device.

Glass Launcher has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in the launcher is tracked, profiled, or sold. The app makes exactly one network request, described below.

What stays on your device

All of the following is read and used entirely on your phone. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no way to see it:

DataWhy the app reads it
Installed apps and their icons To draw the app grid, App Library and Search. This is what a launcher is.
Calendar events (READ_CALENDAR) Optional. Shows your next event on the clock widget. If you decline the permission, the line is hidden — the app does not invent a placeholder.
App usage history (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) Optional, and off unless you grant it in Android Settings. Ranks the Suggestions row by what you have actually been using. Without it, Suggestions falls back to install date.
Your home screen layout and preferences Icon positions, folders, pages, widget choices and settings. Stored in the app's own private storage.

The one thing that leaves your device

If you add the weather widget and grant approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), the app sends your coordinates to Open-Meteo to fetch a temperature and forecast:

  • The request goes to api.open-meteo.com and contains only latitude and longitude, rounded to two decimal places (roughly a one-kilometre area) — never your precise position.
  • No device identifier, account, name, or any other data is attached. There is no API key, because Open-Meteo does not require one.
  • The app uses your last known coarse location; it does not request a live GPS fix and never accesses location in the background.
  • The result is displayed and discarded. It is not stored on any server belonging to the developer — the developer operates no server at all.

Open-Meteo's own handling of requests is described in their terms and privacy notice. If you do not add the weather widget, or decline the location permission, the app makes no network requests whatsoever.

Permissions, and what happens if you refuse

Every permission is optional and every one degrades to silence rather than to a guess:

  • Location refused → the weather tile says so and shows no temperature.
  • Calendar refused → the agenda line is hidden entirely.
  • Usage access not granted → Suggestions ranks by install date.
  • Internet is used only for the weather request described above.

The app does not request telephony, contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, precise location, or background location permissions.

Children

Glass Launcher is not directed at children and is declared for users aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

All app data lives in Android's private storage for this app. Uninstalling Glass Launcher, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes everything it holds. There is no server-side copy to request deletion of, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be noted in the release notes on Google Play.
